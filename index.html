Current chapter

Amazon Leo Government

Amazon Leo is building a low-Earth-orbit satellite network for fast, reliable connectivity beyond the reach of existing infrastructure. My work focuses on the government field-operations side: designing the support-platform architecture, connecting systems and identity layers, and leading engineering teams through a complex operational build.

The opportunity is larger than connectivity alone. Satellite networks, cloud infrastructure, secure data movement, and field support have to function as one service for demanding public-sector customers.

Selected mission work

Programs where technology serves a larger system.

The most important work in my career has required more than delivering a product. It required navigating acquisition, security, operations, policy, and organizational boundaries at the same time.

GPS Directorate2016 to 2017

Modernizing the system the world uses to know where and when it is.

Mission contextGPS is a global positioning, navigation, and timing utility that supports military operations and synchronizes communications, transportation, finance, and critical infrastructure. The Space and Missile Systems Center, now Space Systems Command, was responsible for acquiring its satellites, control segment, and military user equipment.

My contributionOn the GPS Program Executive Officer staff, I supported governance of a $32B portfolio, including $1.9B in GPS III funding, the $5.6B OCX control segment, and a $100M gap-filler strategy. I helped turn technical and program risk into decisions senior leaders could act on.

Nuclear detection2014 to 2016

Turning a distant nuclear event into trusted, near-real-time warning.

Mission contextThe United States Nuclear Detonation Detection System combines space-based sensors, communications, and ground processing to detect, locate, and characterize nuclear detonations. Sandia and Los Alamos provide decades of specialized sensor and phenomenology expertise supporting treaty verification and national decision-making.

My contributionI led a $20M-per-year ground-system program across nine global sites and multiple security organizations. We implemented the Risk Management Framework, forecast 20% lifecycle savings, and delivered a $3M modernization 30 days early.

Overhead intelligence2017 to 2021

Making overhead data usable across the intelligence and defense enterprise.

Mission contextThe NRO builds and operates reconnaissance spacecraft; NGA and other partners analyze and distribute the resulting collections; the military services apply those products to operational missions. The value depends on moving data quickly and securely across organizational boundaries.

My contributionAt the NRO, I led a 55-person, $50M+ portfolio integrating OPIR data and 15 nontraditional feeds, increasing available mission data by 38%. We took a cross-domain platform from concept to a $10M deployment and secured a $26M follow-on.
