Orbital compute and AI infrastructure

Orbital compute changes system design. It reshapes ground architecture, tasking models, security boundaries, and cost structure. When downlink is constrained, moving inference closer to collection becomes an architecture decision rather than an optimization.

I am focused on companies building satellite constellations with integrated data pipelines, orbital edge compute platforms, and space-to-cloud systems where compute placement is part of the product.
